## Problem: Valid Anagram (Easy)

**Link:** https://leetcode.com/problems/valid-anagram/

### Approach

I sorted both strings and then compared them.
If the sorted strings are equal, the two strings are anagrams.

### Complexity

- Time: O(n log n)
- Space: O(n)

### Notes

I tested the solution locally with an anagram case and a non-anagram case.
