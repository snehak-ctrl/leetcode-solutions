## Problem: Reverse a String (Easy)

**Link:** https://leetcode.com/problems/reverse-string/

### Approach

I used two pointers, one starting from the beginning of the string and one from the end.
I swapped the characters and moved both pointers toward the center until the string was reversed.

### Complexity

- Time: O(n)
- Space: O(1)

### Notes

I tested the solution locally with "hello" and "sneha".
The outputs were "olleh" and "ahens".
